## [1.0.0] - 2026-05-20

### Added
- `deslop-ui` skill — UI copy slop removal: button labels, error messages, empty states, tooltips, success messages. Includes AI UI fingerprint taxonomy, word-level replacement table, and three anti-patterns (hedging, superlative decoration, verbose empty state).
- `deslop-copy` skill — Marketing and editorial copy humanization: blacklist of 40+ AI clichés, 10 humanization techniques, workflow with 5-pass structure, and three anti-patterns (synonym swapping, over-humanization, specificity theater).
- `deslop-detect` skill — AI content detection prevention: explains perplexity, burstiness, and lexical entropy signals; covers GPTZero, Originality.ai, Turnitin, Copyleaks, ZeroGPT, and Sapling; provides 5-pass rewriting strategy and pre-publication checklist. Three anti-patterns (synonym spinning, AI-generated humanization, single-pass fix).
