**1. Button:** `Click here to proceed to the next step of the checkout process`

- "Click here" is redundant — buttons are for clicking. "Proceed to the next step of the checkout process" is 8 words for a concept that needs 1.
- **Replace:** `Continue`

---

**2. Error:** `There seems to have been an issue processing your payment at this time. Please try again later or contact support if the problem persists.`

- "There seems to have been" is hedging — either it failed or it didn't. "At this time" is filler. The whole thing buries the actual problem.
- **Replace:** `Payment failed. Try again or contact support.`

---

**3. Success:** `Your account has been successfully created and you can now begin using all of our features.`

- "Successfully" is implicit in a success state. "Begin using all of our features" is corporate puffery that adds no information.
- **Replace:** `Account created.`

---

**4. Tooltip:** `This is where you can leverage our comprehensive suite of powerful reporting tools to gain in-depth insights into your data.`

- "Leverage", "comprehensive suite", "powerful", "in-depth insights" — four consecutive marketing terms. "This is where you can" is pure throat-clearing. The tooltip doesn't say what the tool actually does.
- **Replace:** `Reports` (or `View reports and analytics` if the icon isn't self-explanatory)
