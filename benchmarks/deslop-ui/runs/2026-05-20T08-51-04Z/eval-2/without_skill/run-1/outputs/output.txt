Both pieces share the same core problems: they're self-focused, padded with filler, and bury or omit the action. Here's a breakdown:

---

## Empty State

**What's wrong:**

- **"It looks like"** — the system knows exactly what's there. Hedging is noise.
- **"Don't worry"** — presumes anxiety the user may not feel. Slightly condescending.
- **Benefits list is generic** — "organize work, collaborate, track progress" describes every project tool ever made.
- **Passive CTA** — "feel free to... whenever you're ready" diffuses urgency without adding warmth. It's polite to the point of being useless.
- **Three exclamation marks** — forced enthusiasm.

**Rewrite:**

> **No projects yet**
>
> Create your first project to bring your work, team, and progress into one place.
>
> [Create a project]

Or if you want slightly warmer:

> **Start your first project**
>
> Projects keep your work organized and your team aligned. Takes about a minute to set up.
>
> [Create a project]

---

## Onboarding Modal

**What's wrong:**

- **"This is where you'll be able to see"** — tell them what they *can do*, not where things will eventually be visible.
- **"We've designed this experience to be as intuitive and seamless as possible"** — pure self-congratulation. Users will judge that themselves. Cut it entirely.
- **"Please don't hesitate to reach out"** — corporate filler. Either give a specific support path or skip it.
- **No next step** — the modal ends with a whimper. What should the user do now?

**Rewrite:**

> **Welcome to your dashboard**
>
> Here you'll see your key metrics, recent activity, and team updates — updated in real time.
>
> Start by connecting your first data source, or explore the overview with sample data.
>
> [Connect data] [Explore with samples]

---

## The pattern to fix

Both pieces follow the same broken formula:
1. State the obvious
2. Reassure unnecessarily
3. Describe the product generically
4. Weak or missing CTA

Replace it with: **one clear statement of value + one specific action**. Cut everything that doesn't serve those two goals.
