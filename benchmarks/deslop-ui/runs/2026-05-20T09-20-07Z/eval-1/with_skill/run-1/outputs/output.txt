**1. Button: 'Click here to proceed to the next step of the checkout process'**
- "Click here" is redundant — buttons are clickable by definition
- 11 words for a button label; should be 1–3
- "proceed to the next step of" is pure filler

→ **Continue** or **Next**

---

**2. Error: 'There seems to have been an issue processing your payment...'**
- "There seems to have been" — hedging makes it sound uncertain about its own error
- Passive voice hides who failed and what failed
- "at this time" is corporate filler
- Doesn't tell the user what to do first

→ **Payment failed. Check your card details and try again.**

---

**3. Success: 'Your account has been successfully created and you can now begin using all of our features.'**
- "has been successfully created" — passive and wordy; if you're reading this, you know it worked
- "begin using all of our features" — vague marketing language
- The message doesn't orient the user to what's next

→ **Account created.** (or **You're in.** if the tone allows it)

---

**4. Tooltip: 'This is where you can leverage our comprehensive suite of powerful reporting tools...'**
- "leverage" and "comprehensive suite of powerful" are pure jargon
- "gain in-depth insights into your data" says nothing specific
- Tooltips should say what the thing *does*, not sell it

→ **View reports and analytics.**
