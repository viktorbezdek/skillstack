## Empty State

**What's wrong:**

1. **"It looks like"** — hedging. The system knows exactly what's happened. Uncertainty signals erode trust.
2. **"Don't worry"** — patronizing. Nobody was worried until you said that.
3. **"getting started is easy and takes less than a minute"** — effort-signaling. Users scan, they don't read reassurances.
4. **"Feel free to... whenever you're ready!"** — the weakest possible CTA. It's permission to delay.
5. **Length** — empty states get one glance. This is a paragraph.

**Replacement:**

> **No projects yet**
>
> Create your first project to organize work, collaborate, and track progress.
>
> [Create project]

## Onboarding Modal

**What's wrong:**

1. **"This is where you'll be able to see"** — dead weight. Cut to the noun.
2. **"We've designed this experience to be as intuitive and seamless as possible"** — self-congratulatory. Users don't care; if it needs announcing, it probably isn't.
3. **"so that you can focus on what matters most to you"** — hollow filler that says nothing specific.
4. **"Please don't hesitate to reach out"** — corporate softening of a simple offer. "Don't hesitate" implies the user might feel like a burden.
5. **No action** — the modal ends with a question invitation, not a step forward.

**Replacement:**

> **Your dashboard**
>
> Your metrics, reports, and team activity live here. Start by connecting your first data source.
>
> [Get started] [I'll explore on my own]

---

**Pattern across both:** the original copy treats length as a signal of helpfulness. It isn't — it's friction. Every word that doesn't move the user forward is a word asking them to stop and process. Cut to the action.
