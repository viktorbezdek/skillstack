**Flagged clichés**

1. **"Leverage"** — corporate verb that means "use"
2. **"comprehensive"** — filler adjective, says nothing
3. **"cutting-edge"** — every product claims this
4. **"empower your team"** — HR-speak placeholder
5. **"unlock unprecedented growth"** — abstract hype with no claim
6. **"innovative solution"** — meaningless; all solutions call themselves innovative
7. **"seamlessly integrates"** — tech marketing staple; real integrations are never seamless
8. **"deliver transformative results"** — vague outcome with no specifics
9. **"drive sustainable success"** — double-stacked empty nouns
10. **"Furthermore"** — stilted AI transition word
11. **"robust analytics"** — "robust" is automatic padding
12. **"valuable insights"** — tautological (insights are by definition valuable)
13. **"enable informed decision-making"** — bureaucratic passive construction
14. **"at every level of your organization"** — corporate filler that inflates scope
