**Why these changes:**

- **Subject**: Asks a question about *them* instead of announcing your solution
- **Opener**: References something real they did — shows you're not mass-blasting
- **Body**: One specific outcome replaces four buzzword paragraphs; a named customer beats "numerous companies"
- **Closer**: "Worth a 20-minute call?" is low-stakes and direct; "at your earliest convenience" is what robots say
