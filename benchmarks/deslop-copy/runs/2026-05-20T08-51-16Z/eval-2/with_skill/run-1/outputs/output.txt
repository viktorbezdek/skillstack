- **Body** — concrete time claim (6–10 hours) gives the reader something to react to; specificity signals you've done real work
- **Closer** — direct ask with a specific time frame; "I'll tell you if it's not a fit" disarms skepticism and sounds human
- **Removed entirely:** "fast-paced landscape," "unprecedented challenges," "actionable insights," "unlock full potential," "at your earliest convenience," "don't hesitate" — all clichés that signal AI or template-brain
