Here's a rewrite that earns its words:

---

We started because we kept running into the same problem: companies were leaving real money and time on the table, and the tools they had weren't fixing it.

So we built different ones.

We're a small team — [X] people — and we work directly with clients on [specific problem area]. No account managers shuttling messages between you and whoever actually does the work. You talk to the person solving your problem.

We have opinions about how this should be done. We'll tell you when we think you're approaching something the wrong way. We'll also tell you when we can't help — some problems aren't ours to solve.

We're based in [city]. We've been doing this since [year].

---

**Notes on what changed and why:**

- Replaced "passionate team of dedicated professionals" — everyone says this; it says nothing
- Replaced "cutting-edge / holistic / transformative" — these words have been emptied of meaning by overuse
- Added specifics: team size, location, founding year — these are the details that make a company feel real
- Gave it a point of view: they have opinions, they push back, they refer out when appropriate
- Ended concretely instead of with a vision statement

Fill in the brackets with your actual details and this becomes a page that sounds like a company, not a deck.
