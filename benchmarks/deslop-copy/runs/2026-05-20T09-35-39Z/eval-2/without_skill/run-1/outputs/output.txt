**What changed and why:**

- **Subject line** — the original announces nothing. A question or observation gets opens.
- **Opener** — dropped the landscape/challenges paragraph entirely. It's filler the reader skips.
- **Middle** — specificity over superlatives. "Streamlined workflows" means nothing; a concrete symptom does.
- **Closer** — "Don't hesitate to reach out... at your earliest convenience" is passive and distant. A direct, low-commitment ask ("worth a quick look?") is warmer and gets more replies.

The main thing still missing is specificity about what you actually do and who you're sending this to — that's what will make it feel genuinely human rather than templated.
