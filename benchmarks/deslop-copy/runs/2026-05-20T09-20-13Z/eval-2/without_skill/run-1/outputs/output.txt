---

**What changed and why:**

- **Subject** — The original announces nothing. A specific question creates a reason to open.
- **Opener** — "In today's fast-paced landscape" is the #1 signal an email is AI-generated. Starting with something you actually noticed about them shows it wasn't blasted to 10,000 people.
- **Body** — Replaced "comprehensive platform / actionable insights / data-driven decisions" with one concrete before/after. Specifics build credibility; abstractions kill it.
- **CTA** — "Don't hesitate to reach out at your earliest convenience" is passive and formal. A concrete ask ("20-minute call this week") is easier to say yes or no to.

The brackets are intentional — fill them in per recipient. The more specific those details are, the higher your reply rate.
