**Step 1 — Slop Inventory**

```
Blacklist hits:
- "fast-paced business landscape" (opener cliché)
- "unprecedented" (adjective)
- "Now more than ever" (opener phrase)
- "leverage" (verb)
- "cutting-edge" (adjective)
- "comprehensive" (adjective)
- "streamline" (overused verb)
- "transformative results" (vague outcome)
- "Furthermore" (filler connective)
- "state-of-the-art" (adjective)
- "actionable insights" (vague outcome)
- "empower your team" (empower)
- "data-driven decisions" (vague outcome)
- "Don't hesitate to reach out" (closer cliché)
- "unlock its full potential" (abstract unlock)
- "We look forward to connecting with you at your earliest convenience" (closer cliché)

Structural patterns: opener-cliché, closer-cliché ×2, abstract-outcomes throughout,
no specifics (zero numbers/names/examples), no opinion, uniform sentence length

Original word count: ~100 words
```

---

**Step 2 — Rewrite** (≤100 words)

> **Subject: Quick question about [Company]'s ops**

Hi [Name],

I saw [Company] is [hiring ops roles / expanding to X market / recent trigger]. That usually means coordination is getting harder to manage.

We help teams like yours cut the manual back-and-forth — the kind that doesn't belong to anyone but still eats hours every week. Our reporting also surfaces where the slowdowns actually are, not just that they exist.

Worth 15 minutes to see if it's relevant? Pick a time here: [link].

[Your name]

---

**Step 3 — Humanization Checklist**

- [x] Contractions present ("it's", "don't")
- [x] Short sentence ≤8 words: "Worth 15 minutes to see if it's relevant?"
- [x] No two consecutive sentences start the same way
- [x] Concrete specifics: real timeframe (15 min), specific trigger placeholder, specific outcome framing
- [x] Opener is not a landscape/era phrase — it's a direct observation

---

**Note:** The `[specific trigger]` placeholder is doing the most work here. The original email failed because it had *no* reason to land in anyone's inbox. Every personalized cold email needs a real, specific hook — job posting, funding news, a shared contact, something that shows you looked. Without it, even a clean rewrite reads like a template.
