**Burstiness — the biggest problem.** All four sentences sit in the 11–23 word range. That's a flat line. GPTZero's burstiness score tanks when sentence lengths cluster like this.

**Classic AI transitions.** "Furthermore," "Additionally," and "It is important to note that" are so common in AI output that they appear explicitly in detector training data. They're basically a signature at this point.

**Perplexity is too low.** Every phrase is the statistically expected next token: "essential component," "data-driven approaches," "consistently outperform their competitors," "thought leadership," "customer engagement and conversion rates."
