## What Each Detector Looks For

**GPTZero**
- **Perplexity** — how predictable each word choice is. AI gravitates toward the statistically "safest" next token, producing low perplexity scores.
- **Burstiness** — variance in sentence length and complexity. Humans write in bursts (short, long, fragment, complex). AI produces uniform rhythm.
- Trained classifier on known AI vs. human corpora.

**Originality.ai**
- Similar perplexity/burstiness signals, but weighted differently.
- Flags predictable topic transitions and "corporate scaffold" structure (claim → support → claim → support).
- Has a running list of high-frequency AI phrases ("fundamentally transformed," "attract and retain top talent," "embrace").

## Why Your Text Flags

Your intro hits every pattern:
- Four sentences of nearly identical length and structure
- Zero specifics — no data, no timeframe, no named tension
- Generic positive framing with no friction or opinion
- Three flagged phrases in 80 words

---

## Rewrite Strategy

1. **Open with something unexpected** — a contradiction, a number, a confession
2. **Vary length aggressively** — one-word sentence. Then something long and winding that earns its complexity.
3. **Add specificity** — a year, a company, a statistic, a real observation
4. **Introduce friction** — the popular story vs. the messier reality
5. **Take a position** — not "organizations report higher satisfaction" but *your* read on it
