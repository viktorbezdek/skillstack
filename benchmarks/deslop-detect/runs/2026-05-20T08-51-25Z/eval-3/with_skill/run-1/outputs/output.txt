**Original:**

> The analytics work. That's the part most teams actually care about — not the AI story, not the integrations pitch, but whether the numbers they see on Monday morning reflect what actually happened. **What changed:**
- Short sentence ("The analytics work.") + long sentence = burstiness

- Removed "Furthermore," "transformative," "seamless," "robust," "empower"
- Prose converts the implicit list structure into flowing sentences
