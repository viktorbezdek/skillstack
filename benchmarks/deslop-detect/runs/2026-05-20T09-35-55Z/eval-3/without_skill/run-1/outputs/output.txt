Synonym swapping doesn't work. AI detectors don't just flag individual words — they analyze statistical patterns: sentence-length variation, predictability of word sequences (perplexity), syntactic uniformity, and burstiness. Swapping "leverage" for "harness" leaves all of those intact.

More importantly, that text has a deeper problem: it says nothing. "Comprehensive platform," "robust analytics," "seamless integrations," "transformative results" — these are placeholders, not claims. No synonym swap fixes that.

**What actually works: rewrite with specifics.**

The goal isn't to fool a detector — it's to write something a human would actually write, which means making concrete claims.

Ask yourself:
- What does the platform *actually do*? (not "leverage AI" — specifically what)
- What do the analytics *show* that teams act on?
- Which integrations? Slack? Salesforce? Ten? Two hundred?
- What does "transformative results" mean? Faster decisions? Less time in spreadsheets? A number?

A rewritten version might look like:

> *Our platform pulls data from your existing tools — Salesforce, Jira, whatever you're already running — and surfaces the patterns your team was missing. Analytics update in real time, so you're not waiting on a weekly report to see what's working.*

That's shorter, says more, and reads like a person wrote it because a person had to think about what the product actually does.
