## What Each Detector Looks For

**GPTZero** — two primary signals:
- **Perplexity**: how "surprising" each word choice is given the surrounding context. AI picks the statistically safest next word; humans don't.
- **Burstiness**: variation in sentence length and complexity. AI tends toward uniform sentence weight; human writing swings between short punches and long, winding ones.

**Originality.ai** — similar signals plus:
- **N-gram pattern matching** against known model output distributions
- **Semantic uniformity**: AI text flows logically at every step in a way humans rarely sustain
- **Generic high-frequency phrases** baked into training data ("increasingly adopting", "top talent", "fundamentally transformed")

## What's Wrong With Your Passage

Every sentence is roughly the same length and weight — burstiness near zero. Word choices are maximally predictable: *fundamentally transformed*, *top talent*, *embrace*, *increasingly*. No concrete details, no friction, no voice. It reads like the statistical average of ten thousand corporate blog posts.

## Rewrite Strategy

1. **Shatter uniformity** — alternate short punches with longer compound sentences
2. **Anchor with specifics** — a number, a year, a named study
3. **Introduce friction or a counterpoint** — AI almost never hedges or complicates mid-paragraph
4. **Kill the buzzwords** — every phrase a detector flags is replaceable
5. **Embed a distinct perspective** — not neutral narration, but a stance
